title: "On Having Too Many Tabs Open"
excerpt: "Forty-three. The physical form of my attention."
---
The tabs are projects, ideas, things I mean to read. They are the physical form of my attention: scattered, optimistic, slightly overcommitted.

Closing them all is a small act of self-governance. I do it about once a month. Within a week there are forty-three tabs again.
