title: "The Site Visit That Changed Nothing and Everything"
excerpt: "No drawings were revised. I still haven't looked at the model the same way since."
---
Nothing on the site visit contradicted the model. Every dimension checked out. And still, standing in the actual volume changed how I read every drawing that came after it.

There's a scale of understanding you don't get from a viewport, no matter how good the lighting is. The model was correct. My sense of what "correct" felt like in a body was not.

I don't have a process fix for this. Just a reminder to go, even when the drawings say you don't need to.
