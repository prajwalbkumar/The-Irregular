excerpt: "A dispatch from Sri Lanka, where the plan was loose enough not to ruin anything."
---
We arrived on different flights. Intentional: we wanted the chaos of individual arrival, the small pride of navigating Colombo traffic alone before finding each other at the guesthouse.

Kitulgala first. The river. In the water by 7am, before the heat, before the tour groups. Hiriketiya last — a bay shaped like a bowl. The plan had been two days. We stayed four. Nobody complained.

I have been on trips that were better organised. I have not been on one that felt more like what a trip is supposed to feel like.
