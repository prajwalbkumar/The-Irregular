title: "AI Does Not Make Architects Obsolete. It Makes Bad Architects More Visible."
excerpt: "A position, stated plainly, with the intention of defending it."
---
Architecture is primarily a judgment activity. It is the selection among possibilities, not the generation of them. The judgment requires context, consequence, and responsibility. The tool bears none of these.

The architect who was always mainly pressing buttons will be replaced. The architect using the buttons to express something worth expressing is fine. This has always been the distinction. AI has only made it more expensive to avoid confronting it.
