title: "The City That Forgot What Streets Are For"
excerpt: "Dubai builds relentlessly upward. The problem is at ground level."
---
There are cities designed for walking and cities that merely tolerate it. Dubai, for most of its built area, belongs to the second category.

Walk from one tower to another in Business Bay. The distance between them might be two hundred metres. The actual journey takes eight minutes and requires you to cross four lanes of traffic at a crossing that exists on no map.

The street is treated as residual — the space left over after the building footprint, the car lanes, and the service access have been accounted for. The pedestrian appears nowhere in that calculation.

The buildings will always be extraordinary. The test now is what happens between them.
