title: "Renders Lie, Sections Don't"
excerpt: "A beautiful render can survive a bad idea for months. A section drawing gives it about thirty seconds."
---
Renders are for people who need to be persuaded. Sections are for people who need to be told the truth, including the person who drew them.

I have watched a render carry a scheme through three client meetings before someone finally sectioned the atrium and found a beam running through the middle of the view everyone loved. The render never lied, technically. It just declined to mention it.

The habit I'm trying to keep: section early, section often, and don't let the pretty view get a vote until the boring one has signed off.

None of this is a knock on rendering. It's a knock on sequencing.
