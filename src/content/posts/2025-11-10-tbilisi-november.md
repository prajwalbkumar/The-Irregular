title: "Tbilisi, November"
excerpt: "A conversation between centuries that never agreed on a style."
---
The old city climbs a hill it has no obligation to be on. Soviet blocks next to nineteenth-century wooden balconies next to medieval fortress walls next to a glass bridge from a different decade.

The wine is poured in quantities that assume you are staying longer than you are. November is cold and grey and the city is uncrowded. This is the right time to be there.
