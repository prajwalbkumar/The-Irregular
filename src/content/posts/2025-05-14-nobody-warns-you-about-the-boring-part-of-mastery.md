title: "Nobody Warns You About the Boring Part of Mastery"
excerpt: "The interesting ten percent gets all the marketing. The other ninety is where the actual skill lives."
---
Every field has a highlight reel — the render that wins the award, the script that saves a week, the detail that gets screenshotted. Nobody screenshots the four hours spent renaming layers so the export doesn't break.

I used to think expertise looked like the highlight reel. It doesn't. It looks like doing the unglamorous ninety percent so consistently that the ten percent has somewhere solid to land.

This is not a complaint. The boring part is also the part nobody can copy from a tutorial.
