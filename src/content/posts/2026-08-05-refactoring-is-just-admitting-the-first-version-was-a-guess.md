title: "Refactoring Is Just Admitting the First Version Was a Guess"
excerpt: "It worked. That was never the same claim as it being right."
---
The first version of anything is a hypothesis wearing production clothes. It ships because it works, not because it's correct, and those two words get confused constantly.

Refactoring isn't cleanup. It's the moment the guess gets tested against everything that's happened since — new requirements, new edge cases, the parts of the system that grew up around it while nobody was looking.

I've stopped apologizing for rewriting things. The first pass did its job by existing long enough to be proven wrong.
