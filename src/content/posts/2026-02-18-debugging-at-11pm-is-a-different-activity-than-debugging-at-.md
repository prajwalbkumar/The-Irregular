title: "Debugging at 11pm Is a Different Activity Than Debugging at 2pm"
excerpt: "At 2pm the bug is a problem. At 11pm the bug is personal."
---
At 2pm you read the error, trace the call stack, form a hypothesis. At 11pm the bug has been there for three hours. You have stopped reading the error and started arguing with it.

The 11pm bug almost always requires the same thing: closing the laptop. The solution presents itself before you open it again in the morning. This is not inspiring. It is simply true.
