title: "A Week of Saying No to Meetings"
excerpt: "Declined eleven, kept three. The three were the ones I couldn't have answered by email."
---
An experiment, not a policy: for one week, decline any meeting that could plausibly be a message. Eleven qualified. Three didn't — they needed a room, a whiteboard, or a disagreement worked out in real time.

The eleven declines produced zero complaints and, as far as I can tell, zero missed information. The three meetings produced actual decisions.

I don't think meetings are the problem. I think defaulting to them is.
