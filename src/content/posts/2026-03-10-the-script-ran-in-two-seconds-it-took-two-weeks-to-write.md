title: "The Script Ran in Two Seconds. It Took Two Weeks to Write."
excerpt: "On the honest arithmetic of automation."
---
The script does one thing: walks the model, checks every element against a parameter schedule, writes a report. Under two seconds. A person doing it manually takes forty minutes.

Two weeks to write it. Break-even in five months. After that, pure gain. Automation is always a bet on the future — on the assumption the process will still exist long enough to justify the investment.
