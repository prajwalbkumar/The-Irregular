excerpt: "The moment of convergence in computational design."

There is a moment when the script produces something unexpected and correct simultaneously. The parameters converge. The form emerges. You did not draw this — you described the conditions under which it would appear.

This is not the same as luck. The conditions took days to specify. But the moment of recognition is fast and unmistakable.
