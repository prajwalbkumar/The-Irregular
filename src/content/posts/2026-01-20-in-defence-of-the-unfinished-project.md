title: "In Defence of the Unfinished Project"
excerpt: "The finished project is a closed argument. The unfinished one is still a question."
---
There is a kind of honesty in the project that admits it is not done, that the problem is harder than it looked, that the maker is still thinking.

Most things worth doing take longer than you said they would. The embarrassment about this is misplaced.
