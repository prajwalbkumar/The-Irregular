The short answer is architect. The accurate answer takes four minutes and leaves people more confused. The very accurate answer involves Revit, Python, and a dashboard nineteen people use and two people understand.

I have started saying "I work in construction technology" at parties. Nobody asks a follow-up question. This is the correct outcome.
