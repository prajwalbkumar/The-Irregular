title: "The Difference Between a Draft and a Habit"
excerpt: "A draft you can abandon. A habit has already decided for you."
---
For a long time I treated writing here as a draft — something I'd get to once the real work allowed it. It never allowed it, because drafts are optional and the real work never is.

Somewhere in the last few months it became a habit instead, which means it stopped asking permission. That's the only difference that mattered.
