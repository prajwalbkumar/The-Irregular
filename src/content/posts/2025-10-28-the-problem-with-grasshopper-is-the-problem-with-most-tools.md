title: "The Problem With Grasshopper Is the Problem With Most Tools"
excerpt: "Infinite options, indistinguishable from none."
---
Grasshopper gives you a blank canvas and an infinite set of operations. This sounds like freedom. After twenty minutes it is the specific paralysis of unlimited options.

The skilled user knows what they are trying to achieve before they open the tool. Without the intention, the tool generates forms — interesting, novel forms that cannot answer the question: is this the right building?
